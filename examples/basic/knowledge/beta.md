## Summary

A monthly meetup where volunteers help residents repair household items.

## Hypothesis

Residents who repair items together also want to borrow tools between meetups.

## Evidence

- The first trial meetup filled every seat.

## Uncertainties

- Whether enough volunteers with repair skills stay involved.
- Whether meetup visitors live close enough to borrow tools.

## Decision

Decide whether the meetup is a channel for the tool library or a separate opportunity.

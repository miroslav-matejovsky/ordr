## Summary

Send polite reminder emails to clients before and after the due date.

## Hypothesis

Scheduled reminders reduce overdue invoices without harming the client relationship.

## Evidence

- A beta with 40 accounts cut overdue invoices from 31 to 19 percent.
- No beta account turned reminders off after the first month.
- Reminder emails reuse the existing email pipeline.

## Uncertainties

## Decision

Ship reminders to all plans, or only to paid plans as an upgrade driver.

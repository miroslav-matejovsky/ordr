## Summary

Capture receipts as expenses by photo or email forwarding.

## Hypothesis

Freelancers record more expenses when capture takes seconds.

## Evidence

- In 10 interviews, 7 freelancers forward receipts by email to themselves today.
- Only 1 of 10 wanted to photograph receipts in an app.

## Uncertainties

- Whether receipt text recognition is accurate enough for VAT.

## Decision

Build email forwarding for receipts, or drop expense capture for now.

## Summary

Let clients pay an invoice directly from the invoice link instead of
copying bank details into their banking app.

## Hypothesis

Freelancers get paid faster when the client can pay in one click,
and faster payment is the main reason they would upgrade to a paid plan.

## Evidence

- Median time from invoice sent to paid is 23 days across active accounts.
- 41 of 60 churn survey answers mention chasing late payments.
- Three competitors offer pay links on their paid plans.

## Uncertainties

- Whether clients of freelancers accept a card fee or expect bank transfer.
- Whether faster payment changes upgrade rates or only satisfaction.

## Decision

Commit a team to online payments next quarter, starting with the payment method that has the best evidence.

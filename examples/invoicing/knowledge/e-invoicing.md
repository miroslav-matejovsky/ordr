## Summary

Produce machine-readable e-invoices required by upcoming national mandates for business-to-business invoices.

## Hypothesis

Freelancers will switch away from any tool that cannot issue mandated e-invoices once the mandates apply.

## Evidence

- Two of our largest markets have announced mandates for business-to-business invoices.
- An open standard format exists and validator tools are free.

## Uncertainties

- Final mandate dates and whether sole traders are exempt at first.
- Whether we must connect to national platforms or only produce files.
- How much the invoice data model must change.

## Decision

Decide the scope and timing of compliance work before other invoice format changes.

## Summary

A native mobile app for creating invoices and capturing expenses on the go.

## Hypothesis

Freelancers want to invoice right after finishing a job, away from their desk.

## Evidence

- Eight percent of sessions come from mobile browsers.

## Uncertainties

- Whether mobile usage is low because the web app is poor on mobile or because there is no need.
- Cost of maintaining two native apps with the current team.

## Decision

Decide whether to frame mobile as an opportunity at all or improve the mobile web view.

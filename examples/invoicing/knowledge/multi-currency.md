## Summary

Issue invoices in the client's currency while reporting in the freelancer's home currency.

## Hypothesis

Freelancers with foreign clients leave for tools that support several currencies.

## Evidence

- 27 percent of trial signups mention foreign clients in onboarding.
- Currency is the most requested feature on the public roadmap board.

## Uncertainties

- Which exchange rate source satisfies tax authorities.
- Whether the current invoice format can hold two currencies.

## Decision

Build multi-currency now, or wait until the invoice format is reworked.

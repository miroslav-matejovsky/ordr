## Summary

Generate the quarterly VAT summary from issued invoices and recorded expenses.

## Hypothesis

A ready VAT summary keeps freelancers in the product at the end of each quarter, when churn peaks.

## Evidence

- Cancellations peak in the two weeks after each quarter ends.
- Interviewed freelancers copy invoice totals into spreadsheets for VAT returns.

## Uncertainties

- Whether reports need expense data we do not have yet.

## Decision

Offer a simple invoice-only VAT summary now, or wait for full expense tracking.

## Summary

A shared library where residents borrow rarely used tools
instead of buying them.

## Hypothesis

Residents borrow tools they need a few times a year rather than buy them,
if pickup is within walking distance.

## Evidence

- A street survey found most respondents own a drill used less than once a month.
- Two comparable libraries in other towns kept a steady membership over several years.

## Uncertainties

- Whether residents return tools on time without deposits.

## Decision

Run a one-season pilot from a shared room, or drop the idea.
